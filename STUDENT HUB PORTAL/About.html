About Student Hub Portal

Student Hub Portal is a website made for students. It helps students find important college information in one place. Students can register, login, check attendance, pay fees, register for events and download hall tickets.

Features

  • Student Registration
  • Student Login
  • Profile Information
  • Attendance Record
  • Fees Payment
  • Event Registration
  • Hall Ticket Download
