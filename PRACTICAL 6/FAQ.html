Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some common questions about Student Hub.

Student Hub is a student portal where students can access different college services in one place.

Students can access their profile, attendance, fees, events, hall ticket and other services.

Open the Attendance page and enter your total classes and attended classes to calculate your attendance.

Open the Hall Ticket section and log in using your Student ID and password.

Open the Feedback page and enter your name, email and feedback.
